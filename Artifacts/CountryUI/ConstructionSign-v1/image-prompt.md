# 生成プロンプト

組み込み image_gen、透過背景。板と鉄の留め具を1枚の共用画像として生成し、槌と文字はUnity側の別オブジェクトを使う。

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a reusable transparent 2D game UI sprite atlas for an actual Unity construction / building-upgrade confirmation button, not a screenshot or finished UI mockup. Create exactly TWO isolated reusable sprite components on one wide landscape transparent canvas. Upper 65 percent: one wide horizontal wooden sign body, front view, roughly 4.2:1 width to height, made of TWO broad stacked planks. Make its silhouette visibly a simple stylized wooden sign: stagger the two plank ends, a few large angled saw cuts and shallow uneven edges. The shape must read as timber, not a geometric rectangular frame with a texture. Keep the long middle of each plank completely calm and flat for a separate Japanese button label. No frame around the panel. NO attached metal, NO icon, NO lettering on the wood. Lower 30 percent: one detached small chunky dark slate iron brace with TWO large simple round rivets, vertical proportions about 1:2. It will be reused/mirrored at both ends of the sign, so do not draw left/right duplicates. Put ample transparent spacing between the two components, and leave transparent margin around all edges. No extra assets. Art direction: clear chunky hand-drawn fantasy strategy-game UI, a restrained flat cartoon shape, thick dark navy outlines, only three muted medium-dark warm brown color areas for wood, two matte slate-grey color areas for iron. Minimal broad wood lines only near the ends, no small grain or scratches. Ortho front-facing flat 2D, no perspective. No gloss, glow, specular shine, highlights, gradients, 3D rendering, gold filigree, ornate frame, hanging chains, ropes, nails outside the brace, baked shadows, text, numbers, symbols, watermark, backdrop, mock checkerboard, or sample button states. The actual transparent alpha background is required. The planks and brace are separable source art; a hammer icon and all typography will be added separately in Unity.
