# 共用素材の生成プロンプト

組み込みimage_gen、透明背景、生成1回。PNGの画素編集なし。Unityで切り出し・9-slice・着色を行う。立ち絵枠の縦横比はUI側で1：2に保つ。

Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: PRODUCTION 2D GAME UI SPRITE ATLAS for Unity uGUI, NOT a screen mockup. Make ONE transparent landscape 2:1 sheet containing exactly FIVE reusable, separated components. Flat, clean, lightly hand-inked outlines, simple friendly strategy-game UI, no ornate fantasy decorations. All component bodies/rims must be NEUTRAL WHITE or very light neutral grey for runtime tinting; outlines deep charcoal/navy. No color accents. NO text, characters, icons, labels, arrows, symbols, numbers, logos. No glow, gloss, gradients, light effects, texture, shadows, or background. Actual alpha transparency everywhere outside parts and inside open frames. Layout: TOP LEFT quadrant: one wide horizontal button surface, roughly 4:1 aspect, opaque white flat middle, shallow rounded corners and a strong dark ink outer outline. It will be reused and stretched with 9-slicing for all character-list rows and selected-member cards. TOP RIGHT quadrant: a separate matching wide horizontal SELECTION OUTLINE ONLY, same shape and proportions as the button, white rim between dark outlines, fully transparent hollow middle; no filled interior. BOTTOM LEFT quarter: one simple CIRCULAR PORTRAIT RIM, white band with dark outer/inner outlines, fully transparent middle. BOTTOM MIDDLE quarter: one EMPTY SELECTION SLOT depicted ONLY as an evenly spaced circle of about 24 thick white short dashes, each outlined in dark charcoal, fully transparent inside and outside. BOTTOM RIGHT half: one TALL PORTRAIT FRAME, exact 1:2 aspect, simple straight sides with small clipped corners, white rim with dark outer/inner outlines, fully transparent middle. No stand, clasp, crown, gems, rivets, flourishes or props. Keep the tall frame fully in the bottom half, with ample margin. Every component is separate, surrounded by generous transparent gutters, crisp and usable as individual Sprite rectangles. Keep ALL FIVE parts fully visible, no overlaps. Their purposes are roster row, selected-row rim, circular character-icon rim, dotted empty member slot, and tall standing-character portrait rim. Minimal, flat, immediately readable when small.
