# 遠征画像の生成記録

生成はすべて組み込みの `image_gen`。実際に使うファイルと原本は [image-assets.json](image-assets.json) に記録する。生成画面の見本ではなく、Unityが参照する地形画像と共用Spriteの素材。文字・所有色・国境・経路・選択は別のUIで重ねる。

## 地形の最初の生成・不採用

Create a production bitmap BACKGROUND ASSET for a fantasy strategy game's interactive expedition world map, NOT a UI screenshot. Wide landscape 16:10, overhead 2D cartographic illustration. A continuous inland countryside fills the entire rectangular image, with NO sea and NO coastline, NO empty margins. Simple stylized deformed game art matching hand-drawn medieval resource icons: muted sage and olive plains, broad softly differentiated patches of grass, a few small groups of clearly simplified rounded forest trees, a short band of broad angular slate mountains near the upper right and lower left edges, and one thin winding blue river with two simple stone bridges. Most of the map is uncluttered flat land so runtime territory colors and boundaries can be overlaid legibly. Draw terrain features as simple large shapes with restrained navy contours and matte flat colors, very few details, no shine, no glow, no sparkles, no photorealism, no realistic satellite texture, no parchment. Balanced overhead map composition across the whole rectangle. IMPORTANT: DO NOT draw any national/territory borders, region divisions, country colors, cities, castles, settlements, buildings, flags, roads, labels, lettering, numbers, UI panels, buttons, frames, legends, grids, symbols or watermarks. Terrain artwork only. This will be reused behind 16 irregular interactive region polygons, and must not bake any ownership or selection into the art.

## 共用の城と旗・使用中

Create a transparent PNG sprite sheet containing exactly TWO separate reusable fantasy strategy map symbols, side by side with ample transparent space between them. LEFT HALF: one simple compact medieval stone castle with two squat towers and dark navy roofs, front/top oblique map-symbol view, beige grey stone, bold navy outline, few large shapes, no ground, no shadow, no flag, no lettering. RIGHT HALF: one plain off-white pennant flag on a short dark navy flagpole, clean clear silhouette, cloth shaped into a simple right-pointing swallowtail, large solid off-white cloth area so it can be recolored by the game. Keep the two symbols entirely separate, no overlap, no shared scenery. Simple friendly deformed game icon art, bold readable silhouettes at small size, flat matte colors, no shine, no glow, no sparkles, no fine texture, no labels, no framing panels or button backgrounds, no watermark. True transparent background, no checkerboard. These two pieces will be sliced and reused on every territory and country, not a finished UI panel.

## 真上への編集・使用中

最初の地形画像を編集対象として指定。`transparent_background: false`。

EDIT this background into a STRICTLY VERTICAL DIRECTLY ABOVE TOP-DOWN PLAN VIEW, camera points 90 degrees straight down at the ground, orthographic aerial 2D view. The current image is TOO OBLIQUE; completely remove that perspective. No side faces of trees, mountains or bridges may be visible. Preserve the general sage/olive grassland palette, full-bleed inland rectangle with no sea, and winding blue river. Forests must be clusters of simple round leafy canopy discs seen only from their tops. Mountains must be a few broad, flat topographic rocky ridge patches with radial polygon ridgelines seen from directly above, NOT triangular upright mountain silhouettes or mountains lined up facing the viewer. Bridges must be flat narrow rectangles crossing the river from above, with no visible arch or front face. Make the whole artwork SIMPLER with very few larger flat matte shapes, restrained navy outlines and almost no tiny details or grass stippling. Functional aerial terrain illustration for a strategy game's clickable territorial map. Clear uncluttered plains occupying most of the image. No horizon, no perspective, no isometric or tilted view, no side/front view, no 3D scene, no cities, buildings, castles, flags, markers, borders, country divisions, ownership colors, lettering, numbers, labels, UI, grid or decorative frame. Wide landscape aspect ratio, terrain image only.
