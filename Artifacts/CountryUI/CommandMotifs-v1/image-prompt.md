# 生成条件

組み込みimage_gen。6つの共用部品を1枚の透過画像にまとめた。紙面・封蝋・教本の表紙・片側ページ・巻物の巻き端・しおりを切り出し、文字と既存アイコンはUnity側で重ねる。

## 初回

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE transparent modular sprite atlas for an actual Unity fantasy strategy game UI. Six reusable parts for three real controls: a sealed budget authorization button, an open-textbook training button, and rolled marching-order rows in an expedition list. This is SOURCE ART, not a screenshot, not a composed UI, not a set of complete buttons. Arrange EXACTLY SIX well-separated isolated components in a regular 2-column by 3-row atlas, one component centered in each cell, with large transparent gutters and transparent outside margins. Top-left: ONE blank warm ivory paper sheet, wide horizontal about 4:1, a few broad uneven cut edges and one very small folded corner, calm empty center. No seal or rolls attached. Top-right: ONE small muted brick-red wax seal, a simple rounded scalloped wax silhouette, broad inset ring, blank center, no emblem, no text, no ribbon attached. Middle-left: ONE closed backing layer for an OPEN book: wide horizontal desaturated navy-blue cover silhouette about 4:1, two joined halves with a shallow V at the central spine and angled outer corners, no pages on it. Middle-right: ONE blank LEFT-HALF open-book page block, horizontal about 2:1, gently bowed top and bottom, outer left edge fanned into two broad page steps, inner right edge a simple narrow gutter. Empty ivory page with NO writing. It will be mirrored for the right half. Bottom-left: ONE detached vertical parchment scroll end/roll, about 1:3 width to height, a simple large curl visible at its top and bottom, not a whole scroll, no wooden roller handles. It will be mirrored for the other end, while the top-left paper is reused as the middle. Bottom-right: ONE plain muted teal cloth bookmark, tall and narrow with a single large forked tail, no text or decorations. All six are separate sprites, never touching. Same coherent art style as chunky matte fantasy game interface assets: thick dark navy outline, 2 or 3 large flat color areas per part, crisp simple deformed silhouettes, no detailed textures, no grain, no scratches, no fine engraving. Orthographic flat front view. No gloss, glow, highlights, gradients, realistic shading, 3D rendering, shadows, text, letters, numbers, symbols, labels, watermark, paper lines, background, fake checkerboard, complete button compositions, or extra objects. A genuinely transparent alpha background is required. Typographic labels, icons, quantity badges and animation layers will be separate Unity objects. Make the silhouettes carry the paper/book/scroll motifs rather than giving us generic rectangular frames.

## 透過と輪郭の調整

Edit the supplied six-component sprite atlas. Preserve all six objects, their exact positions, scale, empty centers, thick navy outlines, and the six-part layout. Remove ALL surrounding glow, haze, blur, ambient lighting, shadows, dark backdrop, colored fog, edge halos, and any semitransparent pixels outside the solid object outlines. The entire canvas outside the six crisp outlined objects must be truly empty alpha=0 transparency. There must be no shadow or light under or around any object. Keep smooth antialiasing only within one or two pixels of the actual ink outline. Also flatten the broad fills into 2–3 matte solid color areas with no gradients, no shine, no texture. Preserve the paper shape, red seal, navy open-book cover, blank single left page, scroll roll, and teal bookmark; do not add/remove/rearrange parts. No text, no labels, no mock checkerboard. Output a production-ready transparent sprite atlas, absolutely no background or lighting effects.
